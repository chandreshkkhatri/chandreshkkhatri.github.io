## Lucidity Chat
**AI-Powered Conversational Platform**

Designed a comprehensive AI research companion focused on deep context retention and intuitive UI/UX. Identified the need for complex, threaded conversations and visual mapping to help users manage vast amounts of information effectively.

- **Differentiated UX**: Conceptualized and delivered an interactive Canvas view and Slack-style threaded conversations, allowing users to spawn sub-threads from highlighted text to avoid context collapse.
- **Monetization & Growth**: Defined pricing tiers and implemented a token-based usage metering system with automated Pro subscription management via Razorpay.
- **Performance & Retention**: Prioritized seamless, real-time server-sent event streaming to ensure zero-latency perceived response times, driving user engagement.

🟢 **Active**

## SpikeyCoins
**Multi-Broker Trading Operating System**

A unified trading platform designed to consolidate execution, analysis, and practice for serious retail traders, solving the fragmentation of crypto and equity tools.

- **Unified Experience**: Integrated Binance (Crypto futures) and Upstox (Indian Equities) into a single responsive Progressive Web App (PWA) with a live orderbook and advanced charting.
- **Performance Analytics**: Defined product requirements for an auto-synced Analyst’s Journal that computes realized PnL and weighted average prices to help users track historical profitability.
- **Risk Management**: Designed a Binance Order Monitor feature to automatically trigger advanced stop-loss/take-profit algorithmic orders, protecting user capital.
- **User Engagement**: Built a Trading Gym for sandbox strategy practice and implemented real-time web push notifications for fill events to maximize user retention.

🟢 **Active**

🔗 [spikeycoins.com](https://www.spikeycoins.com/)

## Reweave
**AI-Powered Video Generation Toolkit**

A multimodal content creation pipeline designed to save creators hours of manual editing by automatically converting long-form podcasts into highly engaging, social-media-ready short videos.

- **Workflow Automation**: Identified creator pain points and defined a pipeline to transcribe, summarize, and chunk 2+ hour videos autonomously.
- **Dynamic Content Assembly**: Produced 5 distinct video modes including Ken Burns slideshows and AI-lipsynced avatars to maximize cross-platform social media engagement.
- **Quality Assurance**: Solved critical user UX issues regarding text-to-speech timing drift by implementing precise subtitle synchronization logic for high-quality video exports.

🔗 [github.com/chandreshkkhatri/reweave](https://github.com/chandreshkkhatri/reweave)
